VERS-K2 Quantitative Research Course

A practice-based programme for educators and researchers progressing from quantitative research data to a reviewed manuscript.

Programme starts: 2 November 2026. Ten weeks: eight learning/practice sessions and two research showcase and peer-review sessions. Registration terms and meeting times are available from the organiser.

VERS-K2 · 10-WEEK PROGRAMME

Eight weeks of guided learning + two Research Showcase weeks

The learning pathway moves from credible data and statistical decisions to an evidence-based manuscript. Advanced techniques are selected only where the research question and data warrant them.

WEEK 1

Data preparation

Raw to clean data: variable coding, missingness, outliers, Data Dictionary and Cleaning Log.

WEEK 2

Research questions and analysis plan

Research questions and hypotheses, variable types, descriptive statistics and an RQ–Variable–Analysis matrix.

WEEK 3

Measurement quality

Item analysis, Cronbach’s alpha, McDonald’s omega, reverse coding and an introduction to EFA/CFA limitations.

WEEK 4

Group comparisons and effect sizes

Independent/paired and Welch t-tests, ANOVA, post-hoc comparisons, confidence intervals and effect sizes.

WEEK 5

Correlation and regression

Pearson/Spearman correlations, multiple regression, model diagnostics and the difference between association, prediction and causation.

WEEK 6

Educational intervention data

Pretest–posttest designs, controls, ANCOVA, Group × Time and limits of causal inference.

WEEK 7

Selecting advanced models

Introductions to mediation, moderation, logistic, multilevel models and SEM when appropriate to the research question and data.

WEEK 8

Writing Manuscript V1

Assumption checks, sensitivity analyses, tables/figures, Methods–Results–Discussion and reproducibility evidence.

WEEK 9 · SHOWCASE

Research Showcase I

Defend data and analysis evidence, receive peer review and develop a revision plan.

WEEK 10 · SHOWCASE

Research Showcase II

Present and review manuscripts, assess submission readiness, and prepare Manuscript V2 and a Research Portfolio.

Prepare before joining

Bring a research question or proposal, an ethically shareable anonymized dataset if available, the questionnaire or measurement instrument, and 3–5 relevant papers. Install Jamovi or JASP. Participants without research data may practise on a simulated dataset.

Expected portfolio: clean dataset, data dictionary, analysis plan, measurement/analysis evidence, Manuscript V1, peer-review record and Manuscript V2 with a revision or submission-readiness plan. Submission is not guaranteed.
