VERS ACADEMY · LEARNING EXAMPLES

From statistical foundations to Jamovi and research reporting

The following illustrations are drawn from VERS-K1 teaching materials supplied by the coordinator, not a confirmed syllabus for VERS-K2.

Statistical foundations

Descriptive statistics, probability, distributions, sampling, correlation, regression, confidence intervals and hypothesis testing, explained through mathematical examples and research applications.

Example · Standard deviation

For observations 2, 3, 3, 4, 4, 4, 5, the sample mean is approximately 3.571 and the sample SD is approximately 0.976. No universal SD cut-off defines “small” or “large” across different measurement scales.

Practice in Jamovi: Analyses → Exploration → Descriptives.

Choosing a statistical test

Independent groups: consider Welch’s t-test. Repeated measures on the same individuals: paired t-test. Several groups: consider ANOVA and appropriate post-hoc comparisons, subject to the study design.

Interpreting evidence

Alpha is a prespecified testing threshold; a p-value is not the probability that the null hypothesis is true. Report confidence intervals and effect sizes where appropriate.

Writing Results

Before–after change does not by itself establish a causal intervention effect. Clustered observations may require multilevel or cluster-adjusted analysis.
