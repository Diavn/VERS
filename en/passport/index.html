FREE RESOURCES · COMMUNITY EDITION 2026

Two complementary tools for research development

The Research Passport helps members document current capabilities and track progress; the Handbook provides step-by-step guidance, workflows and practical worksheets.

01 · Research Passport

A 17-page self-managed research development profile: goals, ten skills rated 0–4, evidence records E01/E02, bottleneck diagnosis, a 30–60–90-day plan and progress review.

Self-assessment is not a formally accredited research credential.

02 · Practical Research Handbook

A 17-page pocket guide covering research ideas, literature searching and reviews, research design, data analysis, academic writing and responsible AI use.

Includes workflow diagrams, checklists and five worksheets for researchers at different career stages.

03 · How to start

  1. Set goals and assess your current capabilities.
  2. Choose one research bottleneck.
  3. Use the relevant handbook chapter.
  4. Save completed work as evidence.
  5. Review progress after 30–60–90 days.
