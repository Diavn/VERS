Six research themes (2026–2028)

Teacher education

Professional competencies, microteaching, practicum, lesson study and mentoring.

Educational assessment

Authentic assessment, measurement, psychometrics and programme evaluation.

TESOL and language education

Academic literacy, language assessment, bilingual education and teacher cognition.

Mathematics, science and STEM education

Reasoning, inquiry, conceptual change and interdisciplinary STEM learning.

AI and educational technology

Responsible GenAI, lesson planning, AI-supported learning and teacher AI competencies.

Evidence synthesis

Systematic and scoping reviews, bibliometrics and meta-analysis where appropriate.

Research group matching

01 · Tell us your interests

Register your research themes, contributions, needs, available time and relevant data context.

02 · Identify complementary skills

Match research questions, methodology, data, writing and peer review—not academic rank alone.

03 · Launch a project team

Where a project lead, at least three complementary members and a viable output exist, agree roles, milestones and contribution records.

Community principles

VERS has an open layer for seminars and scholarly exchange and a project layer for members who choose deeper collaboration. Project participation may require approximately 2–4 hours per week depending on the agreement. No membership fee is charged; any distinct paid training or service will be identified separately.

Scholarly contributions are documented with the CRediT taxonomy, while authorship follows the applicable journal and research-integrity policies. Private membership and research data must not be published without appropriate permission.
